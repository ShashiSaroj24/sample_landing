Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Invisalign

Have questions about straightening your teeth with Invisalign? Here are answers to the most common ones.

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear aligners that gradually move your teeth into their planned positions. You wear each set for about 1–2 weeks, then switch to the next set in the series, as guided by your dentist.
Treatment time depends on your individual needs. Many adults complete treatment in around 6–18 months, while more complex cases may take longer. Your dentist will give you an estimated timeline at your consultation.
Most patients feel mild pressure or tightness for the first few days of each new aligner set. This is normal and shows the aligners are working. Aligners are smooth and have no metal wires or brackets.
Invisalign can treat crowding, gaps, overbites, underbites and crossbites in many adults and teens. A thorough examination and digital scan are the best way to find out if it is right for you.
Cost depends on the complexity of your case and the length of treatment. After your consultation you will receive a clear treatment plan and quote with no hidden fees. Payment plans are available for eligible patients, and health fund rebates may apply depending on your policy (terms and conditions apply).
Yes. Aligners are removable, so you take them out to eat and drink, then brush and put them back in. You should wear them around 20–22 hours a day for best results.
